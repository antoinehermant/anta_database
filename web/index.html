Radar isochrones of Antarctica, in one database

The AntADatabase gathers the published internal reflecting horizons (IRHs, or isochrones) traced in ice-penetrating radar data across Antarctica, with their ages, in one uniform format. It is built for ice sheet modellers and glaciologists who need consistent, dated layers to constrain their models, and grows as new datasets of the AntArchitecture community are published.

3D viewer
