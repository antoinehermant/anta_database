This portal is under construction. Please come back later.

Soon you will be able to upload your radar isochrones here, as CSV files, with their metadata (ages, institute, project, radar system, citation and DOIs). They will be checked and converted to the format of the database, and added to the next release after review.

In the meantime, if you have published isochrones that are not in the database yet, we would be glad to add them: please contact us.

Submit your data

Add your published radar isochrones (internal reflecting horizons) to the AntADatabase. Upload your files with their ages and metadata: they are checked in your browser first, then converted to the format of the database and added to the next release after review. You will be credited through the citation and DOIs of your publication.

Need help, or something does not work? Contact us.

The portal is not open for submissions at the moment. You can still fill in the form and check your files, but sending them is not possible. Please email us in the meantime.
